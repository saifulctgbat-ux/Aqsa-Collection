About Aqsa Collection

Aqsa Collection is a clothing brand based in Satkania, Chittagong, built on the belief that stylish fashion should be accessible to every family in Bangladesh. From fun kids' suits to elegant women's dresses and comfortable men's wear — we bring you quality you can see and feel, at prices that make sense.

We deliver nationwide with Cash on Delivery, so you can shop with full confidence — no upfront payment, no hassle.
